Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

1. Introduction

SSJ Creative Graphics ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices and rights you have. By using this website, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

Information you give us: When you fill out our contact form, we ask for your name, email or phone number, project type, and message. This form opens your own email application — the details you type are sent from your device directly to our inbox (ssjcreativegraphics@gmail.com); they are not stored on any server we operate.

Information collected automatically: Like most websites, ours may automatically log technical details such as your browser type, device type, approximate location, and the pages you visit, through tools such as the Meta Pixel (see below).

3. Cookies and the Meta Pixel

This website uses the Meta Pixel, a tool provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. (Facebook/Instagram), to understand how visitors use our site and to measure and improve our advertising on Meta's platforms. The Meta Pixel uses cookies and may collect information such as your IP address, browser and device information, and the pages you visit on this site. This data is processed by Meta in accordance with Meta's own Privacy Policy. You can control cookies through your browser settings, and manage how Meta uses your data for advertising through your Meta Ad Preferences.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and provide quotes for our services
  • Understand which of our social media ads are reaching interested visitors, so we can spend our advertising budget more effectively
  • Improve this website

We do not sell your personal data to anyone.

5. Sharing of Information

We share information only with:

  • Meta Platforms, Inc., through the Meta Pixel described above, for advertising measurement
  • Service providers who host or maintain this website, where necessary to keep it running

We do not share your information with any other third party for their own marketing purposes.

6. Your Rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to:

  • Ask us what personal data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct or update inaccurate information
  • Ask us to erase your personal data
  • Withdraw any consent you have given, at any time
  • Nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of your death or incapacity
  • Raise a grievance with us, and if unresolved, with the Data Protection Board of India

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 9 below.

7. Data Security

This website is served over a secure (HTTPS) connection. We take reasonable measures to protect any information shared with us, though no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

This website is intended for business owners and adults seeking motion graphics and video services. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
